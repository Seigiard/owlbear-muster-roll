- (decision by user) Purpose: GM and players open character profiles during play to see current stats and inventory. Dice rolling helps players not memorise rules
- (decision by user) GM view has a separate tab per character, plus a list of NPCs the GM tracks. A character can be bound to a specific guest. Tokens can be bound to NPCs and to characters
- (decision by user) GM can duplicate an NPC: one skeleton becomes 10 skeletons, each tracking its own token. New capability, not in the spec yet
- (decision by user) Devices: tablet or laptop, never phone. Stakes: hobby
- (decision by user) dummy-sheet interface is acceptable overall; its gap is the absence of sync
- (event) Imported imports/dummysheet-hero.png from the DummySheet store page as the simplicity reference. Coaching path chosen by user
- (decision by user) GM navigation from the owner's sketch (imports/gm-nav-sketch.png): a tab per character on top, then a separate NPC tab after a divider. A character uses its full template. The NPC tab shows all NPCs with their stats on one page, because an NPC has a simplified template and is not a character. Characters and NPCs never share one list
- (decision by user) Sheet and NPC content layout is defined by the preset, because the extension is system-agnostic. UX fixes the shell only and never a hard-coded arrangement of stats or blocks
- (decision by user) Character tabs scroll horizontally when they overflow; the arrow in the sketch is that scroll affordance. The NPC tab is last and always pinned in view for the GM
- (decision by user) A gear pinned to the right of the NPC tab opens Settings in the content area, with import, export and similar actions (imports/gm-nav-sketch-settings.png). The owner asked the facilitator to propose the rest of the shell from the spec
